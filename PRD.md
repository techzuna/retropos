# RestroReserve — Product Requirements

> Generated by `/bootstrap-project` on 2026-07-27. Review and edit — especially §9 Open Questions.

## 1. Overview

RestroReserve is a web app for a restaurant that lets diners browse the menu online and book a table without phoning the restaurant. Diners see live availability, pick a date/time/party size, and get an instant email confirmation. Restaurant staff get a dashboard to manage tables, reservations, and the menu — replacing the paper reservation book and PDF menus.

Scaffolded as a single-restaurant app (one restaurant's own site); multi-restaurant marketplace support is explicitly deferred (see §6 and §9).

## 2. Problem Statement

Booking a table today means calling during opening hours, waiting on hold, and hoping the person answering wrote the reservation down correctly. Menus live as out-of-date PDFs or photos on social media. Third-party platforms (OpenTable, Resy) solve this but charge per-cover fees, own the customer relationship, and are overkill for a single restaurant that just wants its own booking page. RestroReserve gives the restaurant its own booking and menu site with no per-cover middleman.

## 3. Target Users

- **Diner** — wants to find and book a table for tonight or next weekend in under a minute, from their phone, without calling. Also wants to check the current menu (and prices) before committing.
- **Restaurant manager** — wants a live view of tonight's covers, fewer no-shows via confirmations, and the ability to block out tables or closed days. Needs to update the menu (specials, prices, sold-out items) without a developer.
- **Front-of-house staff** — needs to see who's arriving at 19:30, at which table, with what party size and notes (allergies, birthday), and mark reservations as seated / no-show.

## 4. Goals & Success Metrics

- A diner can complete a booking (find slot → confirm) in under 60 seconds on a phone.
- Zero double-bookings: no two confirmed reservations ever hold the same table for overlapping time slots.
- Menu edits made in the dashboard are live on the public site in under 1 minute.
- Every confirmed or cancelled booking triggers a confirmation email within 2 minutes.
- Staff can answer "what's happening tonight?" from a single dashboard screen (today's reservations, sorted by time).

## 5. Core Features (MVP)

### 5.1 Public online menu
Diners browse the current menu on the public site, grouped by category, with prices and dietary tags.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Menu page shows all published categories in the order set by staff, each with its published items (name, description, price, dietary tags e.g. vegetarian/vegan/gluten-free).
- Items marked unavailable ("86'd") by staff are hidden from or visibly greyed out on the public menu.
- The page is readable and usable on a 375px-wide phone screen without horizontal scrolling.
- Draft/unpublished items never appear on the public site.

### 5.2 Table booking with live availability
Diners pick a date, time, and party size and see only slots the restaurant can actually seat.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Availability search returns only slots within the restaurant's opening hours where at least one table with sufficient capacity is free for the full dining duration (default 90 min, configurable).
- Confirming a booking assigns a specific table; two overlapping confirmed reservations can never hold the same table, even under concurrent requests (verified by a concurrency test).
- Booking requires name, email, phone, and party size; optional notes field (allergies, occasion).
- Diner receives a confirmation email with date, time, party size, and a cancellation link.
- Past dates and slots beyond the configured booking horizon (default 60 days) cannot be booked.

### 5.3 Diner booking management
Diners can view and cancel their reservation without an account, via the emailed link.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- The confirmation email's link opens the reservation details using an unguessable token (no login required).
- Cancelling frees the table/slot immediately and sends a cancellation email.
- Cancellation is blocked within the configured cut-off window (default 2 hours before the slot) with a message to call the restaurant.
- The tokenized link only ever exposes that one reservation — never other diners' data.

### 5.4 Staff reservations dashboard
Authenticated staff see and manage all reservations.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Dashboard requires staff login; anonymous or diner requests to any dashboard route are rejected.
- Default view lists today's reservations sorted by time with name, party size, table, status, and notes.
- Staff can create a walk-in/phone reservation, edit or cancel any reservation, and mark status (confirmed / seated / completed / no-show).
- Staff can view any date and see per-slot table occupancy.

### 5.5 Menu management
Staff maintain menu categories and items from the dashboard.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Staff can create, edit, reorder, publish/unpublish, and delete categories and items (name, description, price, dietary tags, availability).
- Toggling an item's availability reflects on the public menu within 1 minute (see §4).
- Deleting a category with items requires explicit confirmation.

### 5.6 Restaurant settings & table setup
Staff configure the data availability depends on.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Staff can define tables (name/number, capacity) and edit opening hours per weekday, plus one-off closed dates (holidays).
- Changing tables or hours immediately affects future availability searches but never silently deletes existing confirmed reservations — conflicts are surfaced to staff for manual resolution.
- Restaurant profile (name, address, phone, description) is editable and shown on the public site.

## 6. Later / Nice-to-Have

- **Multi-restaurant support** — deferred; the MVP is one restaurant's own site. Data model keeps a `Restaurant` entity so this stays feasible.
- **Deposits / prepayment (Stripe)** — deferred until no-show volume proves the need; adds PCI/payment scope.
- **SMS reminders (Twilio)** — email-only in MVP; SMS adds cost and a provider dependency.
- **Diner accounts with booking history** — tokenized links cover MVP needs without password/PII overhead.
- **Waitlist for fully booked slots** — meaningful only once real demand data exists.
- **Online ordering / delivery** — a different product; menus in MVP are read-only for diners.
- **Floor-plan visual table editor** — a simple table list is enough for MVP.

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Correctness of bookings:** double-booking prevention must be enforced at the database/transaction level, not just in UI logic.
- **Time handling:** all slots stored in UTC with the restaurant's IANA timezone applied for display and availability math; DST boundary days must not produce phantom or missing slots.
- **Privacy (PII):** diner names, emails, and phone numbers are PII — never logged in plaintext, never exposed in URLs (cancellation tokens are random, not derived from PII), retained only as long as needed.
- **Performance:** public menu and availability search respond in under 1s on a median mobile connection.
- **Mobile-first:** all diner-facing pages designed for phones first; dashboard optimized for tablet/desktop.
- **Accessibility:** diner-facing flows meet WCAG 2.1 AA basics (contrast, keyboard navigation, form labels).
- **Abuse resistance:** rate limiting on booking creation to prevent spam reservations flooding availability.

## 8. Out of Scope

- No native mobile apps in v1 — responsive web only.
- No payment processing of any kind in v1 (diners pay at the restaurant).
- No third-party reservation platform integrations (OpenTable/Google Reserve sync).
- No POS or kitchen system integration.
- No multi-language support in v1 — English only.

## 9. Open Questions

1. **Single restaurant or platform?** Scaffolding assumes one restaurant's own site. If the real goal is a multi-restaurant marketplace, the data model holds but onboarding, search, and billing become new MVP-scale work.
2. **What is the real restaurant** (name, address, timezone, opening hours, table inventory) — needed to seed the database and configure defaults.
3. **Booking policy numbers:** dining duration (90 min?), booking horizon (60 days?), cancellation cut-off (2 h?), max party size bookable online before "call us" applies (8?).
4. **Should staff manually approve bookings**, or are they auto-confirmed as assumed here?
5. **Branding:** any existing logo/colors/photography, or should design start from scratch?
